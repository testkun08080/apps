## 2. What the App does

The App is an iOS app for turning what you say while looking at photos you picked into a draft article in Markdown. Recording, transcription, editing and previewing happen on your device; only AI outline generation uses an external service. Optional iCloud sync is available across devices on the same Apple ID. See the [Privacy Policy](https://apps.testkun.net/en/shitagaki/privacy/) for details on what is handled.

## 3. What is free

Creating drafts, recording, memos, editing the outline and previewing are free. On the free tier, AI outline generation is limited to **10 runs in total**. That count never resets, and regenerating counts as another run. The free quota is counted on the server by device identifier, so it does not reset on reinstall.

Once the free quota is gone, watching a **rewarded video** through to the end — a video ad that plays only when you choose it — earns one AI outline generation (once a day, up to five a calendar month) or one export (once a day). Closing the video early grants nothing. Earned runs do not expire, but they cannot be transferred or refunded, and a video may be unavailable depending on ad inventory.

- **AI Plus** subscribers are not shown the AI or export video offers.
- Anyone who has bought the **one-time export** plan is not shown the export video, but may still see the AI video after the free AI quota is gone.

- Purchases can be restored from "Restore purchases" in the App, using the same Apple ID you bought them with.
- The AI Plus monthly allowance resets each calendar month and does not carry over. If you cancel, the free quota returns to whatever was left before you subscribed. The one-time export purchase is not affected by cancelling AI Plus.
