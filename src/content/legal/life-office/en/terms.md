## 1. Scope

These terms govern your use of LIFE OFFICE.

## 2. Service Description

The App is a personal tool for recording clock-in and clock-out as a daily ritual. It is **not** an employer-facing time-tracking or attendance management service.

## 3. Pro (In-App Purchase)

LIFE OFFICE Pro is available in two plans. Both unlock the same Pro features.

| Plan | Type | Price (Japan storefront guide, tax included) |
|------|------|------|
| One-time purchase | Non-consumable | ¥600 |
| Monthly plan | Auto-renewable subscription (every month) | ¥300 / month |

- The price shown in the App Store is authoritative
- Pro unlocks the following features:
  - No ads (the banner ads shown on the free plan are removed)
  - Home screen widget
  - Dark theme (and system follow; light remains free)
  - CSV/PDF export and CSV import
  - Customizable barcode content on punch receipts (template selection and saving a custom name)
- Statistics (monthly, weekly and heatmap), note search, punching and complications on Apple Watch, and changing the receipt barcode type (CODE128 / QR) are free
- The monthly plan **renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period**. The renewal is charged to your Apple ID within the 24 hours before the period ends
- Review or cancel the monthly plan under Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions on your device, or from "✦ Pro user (monthly)" in the App's settings. We cannot cancel on your behalf. After cancelling, Pro stays available until the end of the period you have paid for
- Purchases, renewals and refunds are processed **via your Apple ID**. Refunds follow Apple's policies
- Restore purchases using "Restore purchases" in the App. Restoring requires the same Apple ID used for the purchase
